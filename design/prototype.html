Take a look around.

An English browser adaptation of the current Flutter layouts. Fictional data, with no account or backend connection.

What can I try?
  • Nearby: open meetups, select a city or browse the separate places list.
  • People: search people, switch to communities and start a conversation.
  • Walking: inspect a request, offer help or create a demo request.
  • Chats: use the compose action or start with the empty-state guidance.
  • Profile: edit identity and pet details, view saved places and open settings.

Theme, city, tab and saved places persist in this browser. Messages, edits and participation remain in memory. Messages are never sent to another person.

The native map requires Mapbox. This preview shows the app’s unavailable-map layout and places-list fallback. Native authentication, media, live services and all secondary screens are outside this adaptation.

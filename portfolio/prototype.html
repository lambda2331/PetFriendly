Take a look around.

The actual Flutter screens, in English. Explore a fictional neighborhood with an offline mock map.

What can I try?
  • Nearby: move the map, open a marker, save a place, review it or use fullscreen.
  • People: search, start a conversation, join a community or open a meetup.
  • Walking: offer help, create your own request and explore agreement states.
  • Profile: edit your identity, add a pet, view saved places and change themes.

All people, places, ratings and messages are fictional. App changes stay in memory; theme, city and favorites use browser preferences. No live accounts, messages, tracking or paid map services are connected.

Shared Flutter UI and scenario logic. Only external services and the map use demo adapters.
